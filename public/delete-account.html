ConnectHUB — Account & Data Deletion Request

At ConnectHUB, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You can request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request your account deletion through either of the following methods:

Method 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the ConnectHUB app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings > Account & Security.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your decision.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email to our support team:

  • Email: sreeconnect360@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]
  • Content: Please include your registered email address and username.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon processing your account deletion request, the following data is permanently erased within 30 days:

  • Your account profile (email address, full name, username, profile picture)
  • All your uploaded files, media, certificate shares, and posts
  • Your messages, activity history, and app preferences

Data Retention

Non-identifiable transaction logs or system diagnostics required for legal or fraud prevention purposes may be retained for up to 90 days before permanent purging.
